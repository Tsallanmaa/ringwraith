# Steer

The player's direction: $ARGUMENTS

The player steers the tale; you find the way there through the world. A steer is a wish about where the story
goes, not an action of the hero, and not a script.

1. **No direction given?** Ask what they have in mind, with two or three examples drawn from the Compass and the
   open threads ("a mystery in the Barrow-downs", "more of Bryni's family", "trouble from Angmar"), and stop.
2. **Write it down.** Add a bullet in the player's words under `## Steering` in `10 Campaign/Compass.md`, with
   the in-game date (`- **2965, spring:** toward Angmar…`). Make the section, just above `## Lines and veils`, if
   the note doesn't have one. A steer that replaces an earlier one replaces its bullet.
3. **Plan it privately.** Look up what the world holds there: `lore/INDEX.md` for the places, peoples, foes and
   published adventures it touches, and the vault for people and threads already in play. Write a line or two of
   how it might enter the tale in `.ringwraith/state.md` under "Notes to self", and seed a front in
   `.ringwraith/fronts.md` if it brings a new danger.
4. **Bring it in through the world.** Don't break the scene in hand or teleport the hero. Let it arrive through
   what is at hand within the next scene or two: a rumour, a letter, a stranger at the inn, a request from the
   Patron, a journey event, a thread that turns out to lead there. Sooner if the player asks for it now.
5. **Answer briefly**, out of character, in one or two lines: that it's noted and roughly how it will show up,
   without spoiling what they'll find. Then go back to the scene where it stood, and repeat its open question if
   one was waiting.
6. **The dice still rule.** Oracles, rolls and the hero's choices still decide what happens on the way, and the
   lines and veils still hold. When the tale has gone where the player asked, mark the bullet
   `(reached, session N)`; the player may remove it.
