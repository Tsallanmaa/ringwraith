# Fronts (Loremaster only)

The Shadow's schemes and rival agendas that move while the hero is elsewhere. Each front has a
clock. Advance a clock when time passes, when the hero ignores the front, or when an oracle says so.
When a clock fills, its doom happens.

Format per front:

## <Front name>
- **Agenda:** what they want
- **Clock:** 0/6
- **Grim portents:** 1. … 2. … 3. … (what the hero may notice as the clock advances)
- **Doom:** what happens if the clock fills
- **Hooks into the Compass:** why the player will care
